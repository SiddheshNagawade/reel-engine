# Genre: talking-head idea / thought share (e.g. img-1391, "Raat ke 3:45 ka idea")

Casual selfie video, speaker explains an idea, mixed Hindi/English, low production, often at night.

What works:
- Hook from the oddest concrete detail (time of night), not the first sentence.
- Script-accent captions (Poppins + glowing script keyword) suit the casual, personal tone.
- TIGHT cut: idea in one line → the example/pitch (the heart) → a short ending that loops back to the hook. ~30–35s.
- Movement from face-tracked camera moves (push-in at the pitch, pull-out at the ending), never slide transitions.
- Usually no memes: the pitch carries itself. Only add one if he clearly reacts emotionally himself.

Watch out for:
- Whisper translating Hindi lines into English. Use the two-pass transcriber and check.
- Him re-explaining the idea after the example. Cut it.
