# Editing rules

The AI director reads this file on every reel. Edit it, or tell Claude what you didn't like, and every future reel follows the change.

## Cutting
- Remove retakes and false starts. When a sentence is said twice, keep the cleaner (usually the later) take.
- Remove filler-only stretches ("umm", "matlab", "toh basically") when they add nothing.
- Never cut in the middle of a word or an important pause for effect.

## Emphasis (punch-in zoom + coloured caption word)
- 1 emphasis word per 2–3 sentences at most. Pick numbers, results, strong verbs, surprising words.

## Pop-up text
- Only for numbers, money, key 1–3 word phrases (e.g. "₹500", "3 STEPS", "FREE").
- Maximum 1 pop-up every ~4 seconds. Fewer is better.

## Hook text
- 2–6 words, punchy, curiosity-driven, shown for the first ~2.5s. No clickbait lies.

## B-roll
- Only when I clearly mention something that matches an available B-roll tag.

## Overall
- Restraint first. Every effect must serve the content. No gimmicks.
